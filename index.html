    What this system is doing

    This is a practice model that learns only from your labels. You build good replies from small pieces, the model trains on them, and you chat with it to see what it learned. Better labels give a better bot. Careless labels give a bot that sounds sure and gets things wrong.

    1. 1. You labelPick the pieces that belong in a good reply for ten tasks across Facts, Safety, Maths, Instructions and Tone.
    2. 2. The model trainsThe simulator scores your labels against the guideline and teaches the bot what you ticked, right or wrong.
    3. 3. You ask the botAsk new questions it has never seen. Each answer shows how well that area was learned.
    4. 4. You fix and compareCorrect the labels, train again, and see helpfulness change with the same training code.

    The model is simulated and its replies are pre-written. It shows the cause and effect of labelling, not a real AI.
